A self driving car

A self-driving car (or autonomous vehicle) uses AI, sensors, and machine learning to navigate without human intervention. The technology is categorized into six levels, ranging from completely manual driving (Level 0) to full, unconditional automation (Level 5). Commercial robotaxi services like Waymo operate daily at Level 4 in limited cities, while consumer cars offer Level 2 or Level 3 driver-assistance systems.
